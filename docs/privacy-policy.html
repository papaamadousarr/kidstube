Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

KidsTube Code ("we", "our") publishes short educational videos that teach children the basics of coding and artificial intelligence. Our videos are distributed on YouTube and TikTok from a single account that we own and operate.

Data we collect

KidsTube Code does not operate a website or app with user accounts, and we do not collect personal data from viewers of our videos. We do not use TikTok Login Kit, Share Kit, or any feature that would give us access to a TikTok user's data or account.

The only TikTok integration we use is the Content Posting API, which lets our own backend automation publish videos to our own KidsTube Code TikTok account. This access token is used solely to post content we produce — it is never used to read, collect, or act on data belonging to any other TikTok user.

Third-party platforms

Our videos are hosted and viewed on YouTube and TikTok. Any data collected when you watch, like, comment on, or otherwise interact with our videos on those platforms is governed by that platform's own privacy policy (YouTube/Google's and TikTok's respectively), not by us.

Children's privacy

Our content is designed to be appropriate for and appealing to children, but KidsTube Code itself does not knowingly collect any personal information from children, since we operate no website, app, or account system that a child would interact with directly. Viewing and interaction happens entirely on YouTube's and TikTok's own platforms, under their respective child-safety and privacy protections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
